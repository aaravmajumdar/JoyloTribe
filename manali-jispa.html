Manali to Jispa Expedition

Drive beyond the Atal Tunnel into Lahaul Valley: Solang Valley adventure sports, Sissu Waterfall trek, Deepak Tal & Suraj Tal lakes, and stargazing beside the Bhaga river in Jispa.

Duration 3 Days / 2 Nights
Region Lahaul Valley Himachal
Stays & Meals Heated Tents & Hotels
Starting Price ₹7,999 / person

Trip Overview

A high-altitude mountain break away from city crowds. Drive through the 9km Atal Tunnel, witness hanging waterfalls pouring down Sissu valley cliffs, visit crystal clear Deepak Tal, and enjoy stargazing with bonfires at remote Jispa riverside camps.

Package Inclusions

  • Manali - Jispa - Manali SUV / Tempo traveler transfers
  • 2 Nights stay in riverside luxury camps & wooden cottages
  • Daily Breakfast & Dinner (MAP Plan)
  • Atal Tunnel, Sissu Waterfall & Deepak Tal excursions
  • Evening Bonfire & Stargazing sessions
  • Experienced Joylo Trip Captain & driver allowances

Package Exclusions

  • Travel to/from Manali pickup point
  • Solang adventure activities & ATV rides
  • Personal expenses & shopping
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Manali Arrival & Solang Valley to Jispa via Atal Tunnel

Morning pick up in Manali. Drive through 9-km Atal Tunnel into Lahaul valley. Short stop at Sissu Waterfall. Reach Jispa by afternoon and check into riverside tents. Evening stargazing & bonfire.
Atal Tunnel Drive Sissu Waterfall Jispa Bonfire Night
Day 2

Deepak Tal, Suraj Tal & Baralacha La Pass Exploration

Drive towards Baralacha La pass (16,040 ft). Explore turquoise high-altitude Deepak Tal and Suraj Tal lakes. Interact with local Lahauli villagers learning high-altitude living. Return to Jispa camp.
Deepak Tal & Suraj Tal Baralacha La Pass
Day 3

Jispa to Solang Valley & Return to Manali / Departure

Morning riverside coffee in Jispa. Drive back to Solang Valley for paragliding & quad biking. Drop off at Manali for departure.
Solang Sports Manali Drop-off
