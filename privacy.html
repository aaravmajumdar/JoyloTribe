Privacy Policy

How Joylo Tribe collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, phone number, travel preferences, and payment details when you request a quote or book a journey.

We may also collect limited technical data (such as browser type and pages visited) to improve our website experience.

2. How We Use Information

To create and manage your bookings, respond to inquiries, and personalize itineraries.

To send service updates and, with your consent, our Bespoke Travel Dispatch newsletter.

To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share data only with vetted travel partners (hotels, airlines, local guides) necessary to deliver your trip, and with service providers who support our operations under confidentiality.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting concierge@joylo.com.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

6. Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
