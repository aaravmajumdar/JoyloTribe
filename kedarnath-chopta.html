Kedarnath & Chopta Tungnath Circuit

Embrace divine spiritual energy and Himalayan beauty: Kedarnath Dham temple darshan, trek to Tungnath (World's highest Shiva temple), Deoriatal lake reflection, and Rishikesh evening Ganga Aarti.

Duration 6 Days / 5 Nights
Location Garhwal Uttarakhand
Stays & Meals Hotel & Camp Stay + MAP

Trip Overview

A soul-stirring journey into the Garhwal Himalayas combining sacred darshan at Kedarnath Temple with alpine treks to Chopta, Tungnath, and Chandrashila peak. VIP registration, certified trek guides, and heated mountain stays included.

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Drive from Haridwar / Rishikesh to Guptkashi / Phata

Drive along Mandakini river passing Devprayag & Rudraprayag confluence. Check into hotel at Guptkashi. Evening trip briefing and biometrics verification.
Prayag Confluences Biometric Registration
Day 2

Trek to Kedarnath Dham (11,755 ft) & Evening Aarti

Reach Gaurikund and begin 16km scenic trek to Kedarnath. Check into hotel/homestay near the temple. Attend divine evening Ganga & Shiva Aarti with Himalayan snow peaks in backdrop.
Gaurikund Trek Kedarnath Evening Aarti
Day 3

Morning Temple Darshan & Return to Guptkashi

Early morning Abhishek Puja & VIP Darshan at Kedarnath Temple. Visit Bhairavnath Temple. Descend to Gaurikund and transfer back to Guptkashi for night stay.
Morning Darshan Bhairavnath Visit
Day 4

Drive to Chopta & Trek to Tungnath & Chandrashila Peak

Drive to Chopta, the Mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand. Trek 3.5km to Tungnath (highest Shiva Temple in the world at 12,073 ft) and further climb to Chandrashila summit (13,000 ft) for 360-degree Himalayan views.
Tungnath Temple Chandrashila Summit Alpine Meadows
Day 5

Deoriatal Lake Trek & Transfer to Rishikesh

Short trek to Deoriatal Lake reflecting Chaukhamba peaks in mirror waters. Drive to Rishikesh for overnight stay.
Deoriatal Lake Rishikesh Stay
Day 6

Rishikesh Triveni Ghat Aarti & Departure

Visit Ram Jhula, Laxman Jhula, and Triveni Ghat. Departure towards Haridwar/Delhi with sacred blessings.
