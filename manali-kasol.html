Manali & Kasol Escape

Explore the charm of Himachal Pradesh: Hadimba Temple, short hike to Jogini Waterfall, Parvati Valley cafe hopping, and relaxing sulfur hot springs at Manikaran Sahib.

Duration 4 Days / 3 Nights
Transport Volvo Coach + Local Cab
Stays & Meals 3★ Resort & Riverside Camp
Starting Price ₹8,999 / person

Trip Overview

The perfect blend of adventure and chill vibes. Unwind amid pine forests, experience riverside cafe culture in Kasol, soak in sacred hot springs at Manikaran, and explore Solang Valley adventures in Manali.

Package Inclusions

  • Delhi - Kasol - Manali - Delhi AC Volvo transfers
  • 3 Nights stay in riverside camps & 3★ Manali hotel
  • Daily Breakfast & Dinner (MAP Plan)
  • Sightseeing by private cab / tempo traveler
  • Riverside Music & Bonfire night in Kasol
  • Experienced Joylo Trip Captain

Package Exclusions

  • Solang adventure sports (Paragliding/Zorbing)
  • Personal shopping & cafe expenses
  • Lunch during transit
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Overnight Journey from Delhi / Chandigarh to Kasol

Board comfortable AC Volvo bus in evening. Enjoy scenic mountain views as you ascend towards Kullu Valley and Parvati River.
AC Volvo Transfer Kullu Valley Route
Day 2

Arrival in Kasol, Manikaran Sahib & Chalal Village Walk

Check into riverside camps in Kasol. Visit sacred Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara and dip in natural sulfur hot springs. Evening cafe walk in Chalal village along Parvati river.
Manikaran Hot Springs Chalal Village Trek Riverside Campfire
Day 3

Kasol to Manali & Solang Valley Adventure

Transfer to Manali. Visit Solang Valley for paragliding, zorbing, or cable car ride. Evening shopping and cafes at Manali Mall Road.
Solang Valley Mall Road Shopping
Day 4

Hadimba Temple, Jogini Falls Hike & Departure

Visit ancient wooden Hadimba Temple surrounded by cedar trees. Short hike to mist-soaked Jogini Waterfalls. Board evening Volvo bus back home.
Hadimba Temple Jogini Waterfalls
