Leh Ladakh & Pangong Tso

Conquer the Land of High Passes: Khardung La, Bactrian double-hump camel rides in Nubra Valley, Magnetic Hill gravity defying phenomenon, and stargazing at Pangong Tso Lake.

Duration 6 Days / 5 Nights
Max Altitude 17,582 ft (Khardung La)
Stays & Meals Boutique Stays & Camps
Starting Price ₹14,999 / person

Trip Overview

The ultimate bucket-list expedition across Trans-Himalayan desert landscapes. Includes oxygen cylinders onboard, inner line permits, premium heated stays in Leh & Nubra Valley, and lakeside glamping at Pangong Tso.

Package Inclusions

  • Non-AC SUV / Tempo Traveler for all circuit routes
  • 5 Nights stay in boutique hotels & luxury camps
  • Daily Breakfast & Dinner (MAP Plan)
  • Medical Grade Oxygen cylinder onboard for high passes
  • Inner Line Permits & Wildlife Environment fees
  • Experienced Himalayan Joylo Trip Captain

Package Exclusions

  • Airfare to/from Leh Airport
  • Personal expenses, souvenirs & tips
  • Camel safari, ATV rides & monastery fees
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival at Leh Airport & Acclimatization

Land at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport Leh (11,500 ft). Transfer to hotel for complete rest and acclimatization. Evening sunset walk at Shanti Stupa.
Airport Transfer Shanti Stupa Sunset
Day 2

Leh Sham Valley Tour & Confluence of Indus-Zanskar

Visit Hall of Fame war memorial, Magnetic Hill, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, and the dramatic Sangam (confluence of Indus and Zanskar rivers).
Magnetic Hill Indus-Zanskar Sangam
Day 3

Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La Pass (17,582 ft)

Drive across Khardung La, one of the world's highest motorable passes. Descend into Nubra Valley sand dunes at Hunder for double-hump Bactrian camel safari.
Khardung La Pass Hunder Bactrian Camel Safari
Day 4

Diskit Monastery to Pangong Tso Lake (14,270 ft)

Visit 32-meter giant Maitreya Buddha statue at Diskit Monastery. Drive along Shayok river stream to the mesmerizing turquoise Pangong Tso Lake. Overnight stay in lakeside luxury camps.
Diskit Buddha Statue Pangong Tso Stargazing
Day 5

Pangong Tso Sunrise & Return to Leh via Chang La

Witness stunning morning color changes of Pangong Lake. Return to Leh driving across Chang La Pass (17,590 ft). Visit Thiksey Monastery en route.
Chang La Pass Thiksey Monastery
Day 6

Departure from Leh Airport

Transfer to Leh Airport with cherished high-altitude Himalayan memories.
