McLeodganj & Triund Trek

Trek up the pine-scented Dhauladhar mountains: Dalai Lama Temple monastery tour, Bhagsu Waterfall, and sunset camping atop Triund Ridge.

Duration 3 Days / 2 Nights
Trek Difficulty Easy to Moderate
Stays & Meals Hotel & Ridge Tents
Starting Price ₹6,999 / person

Trip Overview

A quick Himalayan trek into the heart of Tibetan culture and towering granite peaks. Walk through oak and rhododendron forests, camp under starry skies at 9,350 feet on Triund Ridge, and soak in Tibetan cafe culture at McLeodganj.

Package Inclusions

  • Delhi - McLeodganj - Delhi AC Volvo transfers
  • 1 Night Hotel stay in McLeodganj & 1 Night Camping on Triund Top
  • Dome Tents, Sleeping Bags & Sleeping Mats provided
  • Daily Breakfast & Dinner (MAP Plan)
  • Certified Himalayan Trek Leader & Local Guide
  • Forest permit & Camping fees included

Package Exclusions

  • Personal porter / backpack carrying charges
  • Personal expenses & Tibetan cafe bills
  • Lunch during trek & transit
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in McLeodganj & Tibetan Cultural Walk

Check into hotel in McLeodganj. Visit Tsuglagkhang Complex (Dalai Lama Temple), Namgyal Monastery, and Bhagsu Waterfall. Evening free time for Tibetan momos & cafes.
Dalai Lama Temple Bhagsu Falls Tibetan Cafe Hopping
Day 2

Trek from Dharamkot to Triund Top (9,350 ft)

Begin 9km scenic trek from Dharamkot through oak forests passing Magic View Cafe. Reach Triund Top ridge by afternoon. Watch golden sunset against snow-capped Dhauladhar peaks. Bonfire & camping.
Triund Ridge Trek Dhauladhar Sunset Stargazing Camp
Day 3

Sunrise at Triund, Descend to McLeodganj & Departure

Witness breathtaking sunrise over Kangra Valley. Descend to McLeodganj for lunch. Board evening Volvo bus back home.
Valley Sunrise Return Journey
