Kashmir Valley Paradise Escape

Immerse in heaven on earth: Private Dal Lake houseboat stay with Shikara ride, Gulmarg snow sports with Gondola cable car, and Pahalgam pine forest valley walks.

Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Special Stay Luxury Houseboat Stay
Meals Included Daily Breakfast & Dinner
Starting Price ₹15,999 / person

Trip Overview

Experience the royal charm of Kashmir. Rest inside hand-carved cedar houseboats on Nigeen/Dal lake, ride the Asia's highest cable car in Gulmarg, and walk through Betaab Valley and saffron fields in Pahalgam.

Package Inclusions

  • Private Cab (Etios / Dzire / Innova) for all transfers
  • 1 Night Luxury Houseboat stay & 3 Nights Deluxe Hotel stays
  • Daily Breakfast & Dinner (MAP Plan)
  • 1-Hour Sunset Shikara Ride on Dal Lake included
  • Gulmarg Gondola Phase 1 Cable Car Ticket assistance
  • 24/7 On-call Kashmir Joylo Trip Concierge

Package Exclusions

  • Airfare to/from Srinagar Airport
  • Gulmarg Phase 2 Gondola tickets & Ski equipment
  • Union Cab in Pahalgam (Aru/Betaab Valley) & Pony rides
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Srinagar & Sunset Shikara Ride

Airport pick up in Srinagar. Check into traditional carved houseboat on Dal Lake. Enjoy a 1-hour complimentary sunset Shikara ride through floating vegetable markets.
Houseboat Stay Dal Lake Shikara Ride
Day 2

Srinagar to Gulmarg & Gondola Cable Car Ride

Day excursion to Gulmarg. Ride the famous Gondola cable car (Phase 1 & Phase 2) to Kongdoori and Apharwat Peak for snow activities and skiing. Return to Srinagar hotel.
Gulmarg Gondola Ride Apharwat Snow Peak
Day 3

Srinagar to Pahalgam via Saffron Fields

Drive to Pahalgam (Valley of Shepherds). En route visit Pampore Saffron fields and Avantipur ruins. Check into riverside hotel along Lidder River.
Pampore Saffron Fields Lidder River Stay
Day 4

Betaab Valley, Aru Valley & Return to Srinagar

Explore Betaab Valley (named after Bollywood film Betaab) and pine forests of Aru Valley & Chandanwari. Drive back to Srinagar for Mughal Gardens tour (Shalimar & Nishat Bagh).
Betaab & Aru Valley Mughal Gardens
Day 5

Souvenir Shopping & Departure from Srinagar

Morning visit to Kashmiri handicraft & Pashmina shawl workshops. Airport drop-off with wonderful memories.
