Vietnam Tropical Discovery

Unravel Southeast Asia's jewel: Overnight 5★ luxury cruise among emerald karst waters in Ha Long Bay, Ba Na Hills Golden Hands Bridge, and Hoi An lantern night markets.

Duration 6 Days / 5 Nights
Country Vietnam (Hanoi & Da Nang)

Trip Overview

A signature international luxury journey combining ancient culture, tropical scenery, and world-renowned gastronomy. Sail aboard a luxury junk boat in Ha Long Bay, walk across the cloud-wrapped Golden Hands Bridge in Da Nang, and ride basket boats through Bay Mau coconut palm groves.

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Hanoi & Old Quarter Foodie Walking Tour

Private airport transfer in Hanoi. Check into 5★ boutique hotel. Guided street food tour tasting authentic Egg Coffee, Pho, and Bun Cha in Hanoi's historic 36 Old Quarter streets.
Hanoi Old Quarter Culinary Street Food Tour
Day 2

Hanoi to Ha Long Bay 5★ Luxury Cruise Boarding

Drive to Ha Long Bay. Board luxury cruise ship with welcome drinks. Kayaking through Sung Sot Cave, cooking demonstration on sundeck, and sunset squid fishing.
Ha Long Bay Luxury Cruise Sung Sot Cave Kayaking Sundeck Sunset Dinner
Day 3

Tai Chi on Sundeck, Flight to Da Nang & Hoi An Ancient Town

Morning Tai Chi session on sundeck over emerald waters. Disembark cruise and transfer to Hanoi Airport for short flight to Da Nang. Check into resort in Hoi An. Evening lantern-lit river boat ride in Hoi An.
Morning Tai Chi Flight to Da Nang Hoi An Lantern Night
Day 4

Ba Na Hills & Golden Hands Bridge Explorer

Ride cable car up to Ba Na Hills. Walk across the world-famous Golden Bridge held by giant stone hands. Explore French Village alpine park and gardens.
Golden Hands Bridge Ba Na Cable Car French Village
Day 5

Coconut Basket Boat Tour & Marble Mountains

Experience traditional round bamboo basket boat spinning in Bay Mau coconut forest. Visit Marble Mountains & Dragon Bridge in Da Nang. Farewell seafood dinner.
Bamboo Basket Boat Marble Mountains
Day 6

Da Nang Airport Departure

Breakfast at resort and transfer to Da Nang International Airport for homeward flight.
