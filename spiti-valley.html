Spiti Valley Complete Circuit

Traverse the majestic cold desert of Himachal Pradesh: Kaza, Key Monastery, Hikkim World's Highest Post Office, Chicham Bridge, and stargazing at Chandratal Lake.

Transport Tempo / Shared SUV

Trip Overview

Experience the ultimate Himalayan road trip across Spiti Valley. Drive through high mountain passes like Kunzum La, send postcards from the world's highest post office at Hikkim, walk ancient monastery courtyards at Key, and spend a night in luxury tents under a galaxy of stars at Chandratal Lake.

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Drive from Shimla / Manali to Kalpa / Sarahan

Depart early morning through pine valleys along the Sutlej river stream. Check into authentic mountain hotel with views of Kinnaur Kailash peak. Evening orientation and group briefing.
Kinnaur Kailash View Welcome Dinner
Day 2

Kalpa to Nako & Tabo Monastery

Enter the Spiti Valley river confluence at Khab. Visit 1000-year-old Tabo Monastery, known as the Ajanta of the Himalayas, and serene Nako Lake.
Nako Lake 1000yr Tabo Monastery
Day 3

Tabo to Dhankar Monastery & Kaza

Drive up to Dhankar Monastery perched cliffside above Spiti-Pin river confluence. Arrive at Kaza, the main headquarters of Spiti Valley. Explore Kaza local market.
Dhankar Fort Kaza Market Evening
Day 4

Key Monastery, Kibber, Chicham Bridge & Hikkim

Visit iconic Key Monastery perched high on a conical hill. Drive across Asia's highest suspension bridge at Chicham. Send postcards home from Hikkim (World's Highest Post Office at 14,567 ft) and visit Komic village.
Key Monastery Hikkim Post Office Chicham Bridge
Day 5

Kaza to Losar & Chandratal Lake Camping

Cross Kunzum Pass (15,060 ft) to reach the sacred turquoise Chandratal (Moon Lake). Short 2km trek to the lake shoreline. Enjoy evening bonfire and stargazing from luxury swiss tents.
Kunzum Pass Chandratal Lake Stargazing & Bonfire
Day 6

Chandratal Lake to Atal Tunnel & Manali

Drive through Batal and Solang Valley, crossing the 9km engineering marvel Atal Tunnel into Manali. Celebration group dinner.
Atal Tunnel Farewell Dinner
Day 7

Departure from Manali

Breakfast in Manali and morning free time for souvenir shopping at Mall Road. Depart for onward home journey with lifelong memories!
